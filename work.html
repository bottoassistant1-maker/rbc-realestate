01Architecture · 2021 – 2026

Architecture.

There is no house style. A project here may be contemporary, contextual, traditional or colonial, depending on the site, the client, the budget and the brief. The range runs from small interventions and individual houses to larger residences, commercial work, residential towers and development-scale projects.

What repeats is the way decisions are made: how the plan meets the site, where the light comes from, which material belongs, what will still be right in twenty years. The common denominator is architectural judgment, not a look.
